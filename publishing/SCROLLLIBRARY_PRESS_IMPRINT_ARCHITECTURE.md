# ScrollLibrary Press — Working Imprint Architecture

**Status:** Working publishing identity for pre-launch production. Not a legal/trademark clearance.

## Recommended reader-facing imprint

**ScrollLibrary Press**

Use this exact spelling and capitalization consistently on copyright pages, ISBN metadata, retailer metadata, cover/back matter, VLB metadata, website pages, press material, and future title records.

## Brand role

ScrollLibrary Press is intended to operate as the publishing imprint associated with the ScrollLibrary ecosystem. It should be treated as a publishing identity, not merely as a one-book label.

Recommended scope:

- AI, data science, software, and technology books;
- business and professional books;
- educational and academic study editions;
- books by Stanley Osei-Wusu;
- future third-party authors where ScrollLibrary Press is genuinely the publisher of record and holds the necessary publishing rights.

## House identity hierarchy

- **Platform / ecosystem:** ScrollLibrary
- **Publishing imprint:** ScrollLibrary Press
- **Author:** Stanley Osei-Wusu
- **Companion technology:** GitHub repository and ScrollLibrary web properties

## Standard imprint wording

**Published by ScrollLibrary Press**

Recommended copyright-page block, subject to final legal/address details:

> Copyright © 2026 Stanley Osei-Wusu  
> All rights reserved.  
> Published by ScrollLibrary Press.  
> First edition, 2026.  
> ISBN: [FORMAT-SPECIFIC ISBN]

The production master must not insert a placeholder ISBN into a released retail file.

## Name-clearance status

A preliminary broad web search on 7 September 2026 did not surface an obvious exact-match publisher or trademark for **ScrollLibrary Press**. This is **not** legal clearance. Before using the imprint as a registered trademark or incorporating a business under the name, perform formal searches in the relevant registers (at minimum DPMA and EUIPO where applicable) and obtain professional advice if needed.

## Operating rule for ISBNs

ISBNs assigned to the ScrollLibrary Press publisher range must remain under the control of the registered publisher. They must not be sold, gifted, or transferred individually to unrelated publishers/authors. They may be assigned to titles and formats that ScrollLibrary Press legitimately publishes and for which the registered rights holder has the necessary author/publishing rights.

Official German ISBN guidance: https://german-isbn.de/isbn/isbn-leitlinien
