# AI Engineering — Pricing and Format Strategy

**Prepared:** 7 September 2026  
**Working imprint:** ScrollLibrary Press  
**Current interior:** 394 pages, 6×9 in, black-and-white

## Recommended launch formats

Launch all three together:

1. **Paperback** — primary student/professional edition
2. **Hardcover** — library, institutional, lecturer, and premium-reference edition
3. **EPUB/Kindle** — searchable digital edition for code/reference use

Do not prioritize audiobook for the first release; the book contains code, equations, tables, and diagrams that depend heavily on visual presentation.

### Why these prices

Comparable professional AI/ML books currently cluster around roughly €40–€60 for print. Packt currently lists *LLM Engineer's Handbook* at €54.99 paperback, while Manning lists *AI Agents in Action, Second Edition* at a $59.99 print MSRP before promotions. This book is broad, 394 pages, technically verified, and includes a maintained companion repository, so it should not be positioned as a bargain/commodity title.

The ebook recommendation is deliberately **€12.99** in Germany because Amazon KDP's 70% ebook royalty band currently extends up to €12.99 including VAT. Pricing materially above that can move the Kindle edition to the lower royalty option, making a higher customer price economically unattractive in many cases.

Official KDP EU ebook pricing reference: https://kdp.amazon.com/de_DE/help/topic/G201645450

## German book-price-fixing rule

Germany requires a binding retail price for a given edition. The ebook is a separate edition and may have a different fixed price from the print edition, but the same edition should not be offered at conflicting German retail prices across channels.

Reference: https://german-isbn.de/fuer-einsteiger/bevor-sie-ihr-buch-ver%C3%B6ffentlichen/preis-und-preisbindung

## International pricing direction

Do not mechanically currency-convert every territory. Use comparable technical-book price bands and local marketplace economics.

Suggested starting anchors subject to live retailer checks:

- US paperback: **$49.99**
- US hardcover: **$69.99**
- US ebook: **$12.99**
- UK paperback: **£39.99–£42.99**
- UK hardcover: **£54.99–£59.99**
- UK ebook: **£9.99–£12.99**, depending on the current 70% royalty ceiling

## IngramSpark caution

Before locking the global print prices, model IngramSpark's print cost, wholesale discount, returns policy, and channel compensation. A price that is attractive on KDP can be uneconomic for broad trade distribution if the wholesale terms are too generous.

For bookstore/library reach, use IngramSpark as the primary trade-distribution route and KDP for Amazon-native fulfillment rather than relying on KDP Expanded Distribution as the central global trade strategy.

## Launch pricing rule

Recommended baseline:

- Paperback: €44.99
- Hardcover: €64.99
- EPUB: €12.99

Do not run an uncoordinated German discount campaign that conflicts with book-price-fixing obligations. Any temporary pricing strategy must be checked for legal and retailer compliance first.
