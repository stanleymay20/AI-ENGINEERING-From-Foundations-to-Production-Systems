# Owner Action Required — Purchase ScrollLibrary Press ISBN Range

This is the first remaining commercial step that cannot be completed from the repository because it requires the publisher owner's legal/account/billing details and payment.

## Recommended action

Purchase the **1,000 ISBN allocation** from the official German ISBN Agency / MVB.

Official site: https://german-isbn.de/

The current public price page displays **€400 + VAT** for 1,000 ISBNs and is marked **Stand 01.08.2022**. Confirm the live checkout amount before payment.

## Use this publishing identity

**Working imprint:** ScrollLibrary Press

The legal account/publisher details entered with MVB should be accurate and consistent with the entity/person that will hold the publishing rights and ISBN range.

## Do not put these private details in GitHub

Enter them only in the official MVB account/checkout:

- legal publisher/rights-holder name;
- postal address;
- telephone;
- billing details;
- VAT/tax details where applicable;
- payment information.

## After MVB issues the range

Return only the non-secret publishing information needed for production:

- assigned ISBN publisher prefix/range;
- the first available ISBNs or range list;
- exact publisher/imprint wording accepted in the registration.

Then production can immediately:

1. reserve three ISBNs for paperback, hardcover and EPUB;
2. update `publishing/ISBN_ALLOCATION_LEDGER_TEMPLATE.csv`;
3. insert format identifiers into final metadata;
4. rebuild final copyright/imprint pages;
5. update the EPUB package metadata;
6. generate final paperback and hardcover cover wraps/barcodes;
7. prepare VLB/KDP/Ingram title records;
8. run the final release gates.

## Important

Do not publish or publicly advertise an ISBN against a product until the format/title assignment is confirmed. Once bibliographic metadata has been made public for an ISBN, do not repurpose that ISBN for another product.
